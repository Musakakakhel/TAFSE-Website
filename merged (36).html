The Real Problem

You keep adding people.
The number still doesn't move.

More headcount. More pressure. More pipeline reviews that change nothing. The issue is never the people. It's three gaps that quietly prevent every sales organisation from scaling regardless of how talented the team inside it.

The Clarity Gap

A deal you would have forecast at ninety percent quietly disappears, and not one person can tell you why. "Qualified" means something different to every rep in the room. No shared language, no agreed stages, and a CRM that reports the past instead of coaching the future. Nobody knows what "good" looks like, so good never becomes consistent.

Typically the lowest-scoring category

The Capability Gap

Your team can sell when things go well. But forecasts are volatile. Objections stall deals. Discounts appear before the client even asks. Managers are firefighting instead of coaching. The execution breaks under pressure.

10–15% revenue lost to discount escalation

The Culture Gap

Accountability is personality-dependent. High performers carry the team while low standards are tolerated. No coaching cadence. No shared ownership. When the top rep leaves so does the revenue.

Widest spread hardest to see from the top
